01 / Our philosophy

Trust deliberately. Enforce predictably. Keep systems understandable.

Three principles. One way of working.

01

Trust
deliberately.

Make trust a decision. Know what gets access, why it needs it, and where its authority stops.

02

Enforce
predictably.

Put the rules where actions happen. Define permissions, limits, and what happens when a check fails.

03

Keep systems
understandable.

Make boundaries, dependencies, and failure paths clear enough to question—and practical enough to operate.
