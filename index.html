01 / Our philosophy

Trust deliberately. Enforce predictably. Keep systems understandable.

Three principles. One way of working.

01

Trust
deliberately.

Know which people, services, and tools have access, what they can reach, and why.

02

Enforce
predictably.

Put checks where actions happen. Make the expected behavior and failure paths visible.

03

Keep systems
understandable.

Leave a teammate able to trace a decision, operate the system, and challenge its assumptions.
