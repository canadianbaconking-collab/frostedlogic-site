About Frosted//Logic

An independent studio building developer microtools, instruments, and interactive systems.

Frosted//Logic focuses on practical software that reduces cognitive drag in day-to-day development work. We favor clarity in interaction, determinism where possible, and local-first behavior when it improves trust, reliability, and speed. The goal is not to ship the most features; it is to ship useful systems that remain understandable under pressure.

Microtools solve problems. Instruments discover which problems are worth solving. Interactive systems act as exploratory environments where those ideas can be tested through constraints, feedback loops, and consequences you can observe directly. Together, these categories create a tighter cycle between tactical execution, strategic discovery, and experimentation.
