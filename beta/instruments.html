Instruments

Intelligence systems that produce signal, leverage, and decision clarity across software work.

Less noise. Better calls.

What an instrument is

Instruments don’t just solve a single task. They help you see what matters—patterns, risk, trajectory, and decision leverage—so you can act with less guesswork.

How instruments relate to Microtools

“Microtools solve problems. Instruments discover which problems are worth solving.”

Instruments often emit actionable outputs: prompt packs, run artifacts, warnings, and decisions. Sometimes those outputs harden into Microtools.

AlphaTrace

AlphaTrace is an internal research instrument for detecting early signals, structural shifts, and hidden leverage inside evolving markets. It traces patterns across fragmented data to surface meaningful inflection points before they become obvious. Designed for disciplined decision-making, it prioritizes signal clarity over noise, narrative, or hype.
