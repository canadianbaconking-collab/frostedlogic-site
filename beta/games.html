Games

Interactive systems exploring constraints, feedback loops, and decision-making—built with the same clarity-first principles as our tools.

Mechanics are philosophy you can play.

Why games exist here

Games are a different kind of instrument: they let you feel systems, not just reason about them. For Frosted//Logic, they’re a space to explore progression, constraints, learning curves, and the shape of decisions—without pretending they’re productivity software.

What to expect

  • readable mechanics over spectacle
  • strong feedback loops
  • deliberate constraints
  • experimentation without hype

Current work

Fracture Protocol

A compact, system-driven project exploring constraint, recovery, and failure states. Built to make mechanics readable and consequences honest.

The Iron Outlaw

A progression-focused experiment about momentum, load, and tradeoffs under pressure. Less spectacle, more signal—designed to feel deliberate.

Cascadia

A fast, replayable arcade-style foundation used to test pacing, feedback, and run structure. A practical sandbox for evolving mechanics without bloating scope.
