Independent builder / Victoria, BC

Richard McMillan

Frosted//Logic is where I build focused developer tools, explore research instruments, and work through questions about how systems behave and what they trust.

Victoria has people building software, AI workflows, and technology that meets the physical world. I’m interested in comparing notes with those builders: showing the work, listening to what breaks in practice, and finding a useful question to investigate together.

My security focus is growing from that systems perspective. I ask what an application or agent can access, where its rules are enforced, and whether another person can understand its behavior. A tool or conversation has a specific scope; it is not a claim of a completed security assessment.

Our security philosophy

Trust deliberately.
Enforce predictably.
Keep systems understandable.

Trust deliberately. Make permissions and dependencies explicit. Know where authority starts and stops.

Enforce predictably. Define controls that apply at the point of action, including what should happen when they fail.

Keep systems understandable. Leave the next person able to reason about the system, operate it, and challenge its assumptions.
