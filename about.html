Richard McMillan

Frosted//Logic is my home for technical work: developer tools, research instruments, and a growing focus on modern IT security.

I approach a system by asking what it depends on, what it permits, and how its behavior can be understood and checked. That perspective connects the tools I build with the security questions I explore.

The scope matters. A focused tool is not a complete security assessment, and a good-looking diagram is not proof that a control works. Any engagement starts with a discussion of the problem, the evidence available, and what I can usefully contribute.

Our security philosophy

Trust deliberately.
Enforce predictably.
Keep systems understandable.

Trust deliberately. Make permissions and dependencies explicit. Know where authority starts and stops.

Enforce predictably. Define controls that apply at the point of action, including what should happen when they fail.

Keep systems understandable. Leave the next person able to reason about the system, operate it, and challenge its assumptions.
