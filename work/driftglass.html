03 / What this means for a review

A green replay result cannot establish that a policy grants no additional authority. Pair historical checks with a policy comparison, then review the newly allowed cases before deployment.

What this establishes — and its limits

Exact comparison applies only to the supported policy grammar and a completed analysis. Supplied events and mappings are trusted; omitted or altered actions cannot be detected from the trace alone. This is policy evaluation, not live interception. Production-export compatibility remains unverified.

This is an independently developed engineering example, not a client engagement or production validation.
